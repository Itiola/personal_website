About

I am Babatunde Fortune Itiola (BFI), a thinker, teacher, speaker, consultant, and lifelong learner passionate about the development of people, organizations, economies and societies.

My work sits at the intersection of technology, economics, and data, where I explore how systems can be understood, improved, and transformed to create better outcomes for individuals, businesses, communities, and nations. I believe that many of the challenges we face are ultimately systems challenges, and that meaningful progress begins with understanding how people, incentives, institutions, and technology interact.

As a consultant and thought leader, I help leaders and organizations navigate complexity, make informed decisions, and adapt to a rapidly changing world. My approach combines analytical rigor, strategic thinking, and practical insight to turn complex ideas into clear and actionable solutions.

Beyond consulting, I am deeply passionate about people development. I enjoy helping people grow, think more clearly, solve meaningful problems, and realize their potential. Whether through public speaking, writing, mentoring, teaching, or advisory engagements, I am driven by the opportunity to share knowledge, challenge assumptions, and inspire positive action.

I am currently building a consulting company focused on helping organizations leverage technology, data, and strategic thinking to create sustainable value. While business building is an important part of my journey, I view it as a platform for a broader mission: empowering people and organizations to thrive in an increasingly interconnected and technology-driven world.

I believe that knowledge becomes valuable when it is shared, wisdom emerges when knowledge is applied, and lasting impact is created when people are empowered to grow. Through my work, I seek to contribute to that growth by helping others better understand the systems that shape their lives, organizations, economies, and futures.

When I am not consulting, speaking, or writing, I spend my time exploring emerging trends in technology, economics, leadership, and human development, always searching for ideas that can help people make better decisions and build a better future.
